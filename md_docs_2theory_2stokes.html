Stokes

Status: third solver in the v1 sequence (ADR 0001, 0004).

1. Governing equations

Steady Stokes flow:

\[-\nabla p + \mu\nabla^2\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{f}, \qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0, \]

with \(p\) [Pa], \(\mu\) [Pa·s] and \(\mathbf{f}\) [N/m³]. For constant viscosity the vector Laplacian is

\[\nabla^2\mathbf{u} = \nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}) - \nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf{u}) = -\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf{u}) \]

when the flow is divergence-free. The solver uses the component-wise Laplacian for simplicity; the vector identity is recorded for the theory discussion.

The Stokes problem is a saddle-point system: the pressure is the Lagrange multiplier that enforces continuity. It has no time derivative, so it tests the elliptic operators and the pressure coupling without the complication of nonlinear convection or time accuracy.

2. Boundary conditions

  • no-slip walls: \(\mathbf{u}=0\);
  • moving wall: \(\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{U}_{\mathrm{wall}}\);
  • inlet: fixed velocity profile;
  • outlet: fixed pressure or zero-gradient velocity;
  • periodic patches: mapped neighbours.

The pressure is determined up to a constant for all-Neumann boundaries. The solver fixes the null space (PETSc MatNullSpace), and the diagnostics report the mean pressure.

3. Solution method: SIMPLE

  1. Guess \(p^*\) and \(\mathbf{u}^*\).
  2. Solve the vector momentum equation:

    \[ a_P\mathbf{u}_P = \sum_N a_N\mathbf{u}_N + \mathbf{b}_P - V_P(\nabla p^*)_P. \]

  3. Compute face fluxes with Rhie–Chow.
  4. Solve the pressure-correction equation:

    \[ \nabla\cdot\left(\frac{\Delta t}{\rho}\nabla p'\right) = \nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}^*. \]

  5. Correct velocity and pressure:

    \[ \mathbf{u} = \mathbf{u}^* - \frac{\Delta t}{\rho}\nabla p', \qquad p = p^* + \alpha_p p'. \]

  6. Under-relax and repeat until the momentum residual, continuity residual and pressure-correction residual are below tolerance.

For steady Stokes there is no physical \(\Delta t\); it is a pseudo-time factor in the pressure-correction equation. The choice of \(\Delta t\) and the under-relaxation factors is documented in the case file and in the validation record.

4. Analytical validation

Plane Poiseuille flow

Between plates at \(y=0\) and \(y=H\), driven by a constant pressure gradient \(G=-dp/dx\):

\[u(y) = \frac{G}{2\mu}y(H-y), \qquad v=0. \]

Plane Couette flow

Lower plate at rest, upper plate moving at \(U\):

\[u(y) = U\frac{y}{H},\qquad v=0,\qquad p=\mathrm{constant}. \]

Lid-driven cavity at \(Re\to0\)

The Stokes limit of the cavity. It tests the pressure coupling in a closed domain with a singular lid corner. Compare against a converged reference or a very-low-Reynolds Navier–Stokes solution.

Manufactured solution

Choose a smooth divergence-free \(\mathbf{u}\), compute \(p\) from the momentum equation, and verify second-order convergence on 2D/3D hex meshes.
