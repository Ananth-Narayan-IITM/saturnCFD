Detailed Description

saturnCFD logo

The primary mark is a compact Saturn emblem above the terminal-style lockup:

[Saturn]
> saturnCFD

The planet is deep orange, the ring and > prompt are green, and the wordmark uses the project's monospace face. The SVG and PDF have transparent backgrounds.

Design

  • Saturn emblem: deep-orange planet (#bf3f12) with an orange-red rim (#ec5b2b) and green ring segments (#00b32d / #00ff40).
  • Wordmark: saturnCFD, with a green > prompt and CFD.
  • The light/default version uses dark text on a transparent background; the dark version uses white saturn and bright green CFD on a black background.
  • No trailing cursor: > is the prompt.
  • The wordmark is outlined to paths, so no font installation is required.

Usage

In LaTeX, use the vector PDF:

\includegraphics[width=8cm]{saturnCFD.pdf}

For a dark slide or terminal page, use saturnCFD-Dark.png; keep clear space around the lockup of at least one planet radius. Do not recolour, stretch or crop the mark.
