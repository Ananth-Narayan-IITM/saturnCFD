Detailed Description

template<class MemorySpace>
struct saturn::LocalMeshData< MemorySpace >

Device-copyable geometry for one local structured block.

Views are indexed by global (i,j,k) through the local ghost origin, exactly like CellField. Geometry is filled for owned cells; ghost cells are left untouched and must not be read.
