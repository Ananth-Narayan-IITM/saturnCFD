Heat / convection–diffusion validation

Solver: saturnHeat (scalar ScalarSolver).

1. Case

Periodic box \([0,1]^d\), prescribed uniform velocity \(\mathbf{a}\), thermal diffusivity \(\alpha\), no source. The exact Fourier mode is

\[T(\mathbf{x},t) = A\sin\!\left(\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x}-(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{k})t\right) \exp\!\left(-\alpha|\mathbf{k}|^2t\right). \]

case domain \(\mathbf{a}\) \(\alpha\) \(\mathbf{k}\) \(A\) end time
2D \([0,1]^2\) \((1,\;0.5,\;0)\) 0.01 \(2\pi(1,1,0)\) 1 0.2 s
3D \([0,1]^3\) \((1,\;0.5,\;0.25)\) 0.01 \(2\pi(1,1,1)\) 1 0.1 s

Boundary conditions are periodic. The initial condition is the exact field at \(t=0\).

2. Numerics

  • Cell-centred finite volume, structured box mesh.
  • Convective flux: MUSCL reconstruction with the van Leer limiter.
  • Diffusion: corrected face-normal Laplacian from finiteVolume.md.
  • Time: SSPRK3 with adaptive dt, CFL 0.3, dtMax from the case.
  • Stopping criterion: the stability-limited step reaches time.end.

3. Reference

The Fourier mode above satisfies the continuous equation exactly, so the reference is analytic. It tests the phase (convection) and the amplitude (diffusion) independently.

5. Observations

  • The error field is smooth and oscillatory at the truncation-error scale; no limiter clipping or odd–even decoupling is visible.
  • Advection controls the phase error and diffusion controls the amplitude; refining the mesh reduces both at second order.
  • The periodic halo and rank-0 local-geometry distribution are exercised by the MPI targets without a change in the norms.

  • The explicit stability limit is

    \[ \Delta t\le\min\!\left(\frac{\mathrm{CFL}\,h}{|\mathbf{a}|},\; \frac{\mathrm{safety}\,h^2}{2\alpha d}\right), \]

    and SSPRK3 keeps the diffusion term stable at the default safety factor.

  • Checkpoint/restart through the generic ScalarSolver path reproduces the uninterrupted run to roundoff.
