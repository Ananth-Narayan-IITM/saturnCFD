Theory pages

This directory is the derivation and validation record for v1. A solver is not complete until its page here contains the equations, the discretisation, the validation results and the observations.

Equation policy

Each page gives the dimensional equation first. A dimensionless form is given only when it is used for a benchmark, and the reference scales are stated explicitly. See units.md.

Validation record

Each page has a Validation section with:

  • case definition and reference;
  • mesh, scheme, time step and tolerances;
  • a table of computed versus reference values;
  • observed order of accuracy;
  • Observations — what the run actually showed, including artefacts and failures. This is deliberately a lab notebook, not a success-only summary.
