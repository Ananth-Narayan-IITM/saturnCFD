Detailed Description

Geometry of a uniform Cartesian block.

This is the first device geometry. It is deliberately uniform: the manufactured-solution tests on a uniform hex grid can then verify the halo and the discrete Laplacian independently of mesh-quality corrections. A general LocalMesh extracted from Mesh replaces it for non-uniform grids.
